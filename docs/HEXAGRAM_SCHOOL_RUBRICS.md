# Hexagram school rubrics

## Shared synthesis contract

Each school record answers the same editorial questions without collapsing the schools together:
what situation is described, what tension or distortion is emphasized, what capacity is available,
and how the source understands development. The essence is 12–24 words. The summary is 90–140
words in 4–6 sentences. Every sentence is supported by at least one eligible chunk.

Summaries use original prose, never predict an outcome, never tell the reader what they must do,
never present symbolic material as a diagnosis, and never reproduce a distinctive source passage.
Differences among registered sources are surfaced as scope tensions instead of silently harmonized.

## Daoist

Emphasize configuration, function, timing, non-contention, and transformation. Wang Bi's
structural and philosophical interpretation remains distinct from Liu Yiming's internal-alchemical
application. Later alchemical vocabulary must not be attributed to Wang Bi.

## Buddhist

Emphasize conditioning, attachment, awareness, compassion, and skillful response only where the
registered commentary supports them. Do not reduce emptiness to nihilism or karma to punishment.
Misidentified chunks are unavailable, not opportunities for reconstruction.

## Confucian

Emphasize cultivated character, position, relationship, responsibility, ritual measure, and
workable order. Avoid moral condemnation and avoid treating cultivated order as mere obedience.

## Psychological

Emphasize attention, expectation, affect, defense, relationship, projection, and integration.
Modern readings are labeled modern and descriptive. They do not diagnose a user or claim to be the
single ancient meaning.

## Human Design

Label the lens as a modern system. Gate, harmonic Gate, channel, center, circuit, and functional
themes require direct source support. A Gate description never establishes personal activation or
a complete channel. Individual readings require line, planet, authority, definition, and
whole-chart context.

## Gene Keys

Label the lens as a modern system. The canonical Shadow, Gift, and Siddhi names may appear as
metadata and thematic vocabulary. The paragraph remains original prose. A Siddhi is an aspirational
contemplative horizon, not a promise, diagnosis, rank, or claim about the reader.
