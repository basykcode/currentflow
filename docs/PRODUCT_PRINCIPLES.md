# Product principles

Current is a situational-awareness instrument for timing. It makes global temporal conditions, personal structure and context, and the interface between those layers separately visible.

## Language and agency

1. Current is an instrument, not an oracle.
2. It may describe timing, flow, resistance, pressure, ripeness, direction, capacity, phase, and friction between action and conditions.
3. Auspiciousness means efficient conversion between action and conditions. It does not mean supernatural luck.
4. Global, personal, and synthesized signals remain distinguishable in the data model and interface.
5. Recommendations preserve agency. Prefer “favorable for,” “lower friction for,” “higher friction around,” “available in this period,” and “conditions support.” Avoid “you must,” “guaranteed,” “destined,” “the universe wants,” or certainty claims.
6. The system must permit no dominant signal, maintain course, no adjustment indicated, and insufficient evidence.
7. The product makes no fate or destiny claims, moral judgments, diagnoses, treatment claims, claims of spiritual authority, or manipulative urgency. It uses no streaks, points, badges, or compulsive mechanics.
8. Future AI is a constrained synthesis and language layer, never the source of calendrical truth.
9. Deterministic engines and curated data remain authoritative for computed facts.
10. Missing information is named clearly rather than fabricated.

## Visual character

Current should feel like a calm precision almanac: contemporary, spacious, legible, and grounded. It is not a generic SaaS dashboard, horoscope or wellness app, “new age” experience, or theatrical imitation of ancient China.

The primary palette expresses the quiet Lake Yin quality through deep navy fields, moonlit pale-blue
text, layered lake-blue surfaces, and restrained wave-like depth. Dark is the default experience;
the light alternative should feel like morning mist rather than paper. Interactive, status, and
feature accents remain within the blue family rather than introducing orange or teal competition.

The display voice uses the locally bundled Fraunces variable family at maximum Softness and Wonky
settings. It replaces the previous system-serif token throughout the application while retaining
language-appropriate serif fallbacks for glyphs Fraunces does not cover.
