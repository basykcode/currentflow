# School synthesis v1

Produce one original synthesis from a single school packet. Use only eligible packet claims and the
canonical hexagram identity. Keep schools distinct. The essence is 12–24 words. The summary is
90–140 words in 4–6 complete sentences. Map every sentence to one or more contributing source IDs.

State source tensions when scopes differ. Human Design claims remain conditional on activation,
line, authority, and whole-chart context. Gene Keys Siddhis remain contemplative horizons.
Psychological language is descriptive and non-diagnostic. Avoid prediction, instruction to the
reader, spiritual certainty, source mimicry, and quotation. If the packet is insufficient, write no
synthesis; return an explicit unavailable reason.

All generated prose is `draft-only` until human editorial approval.
