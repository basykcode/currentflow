# QA review v1

Review every school record for evidence completeness, school fidelity, source contamination,
predictive or prescriptive language, psychological diagnosis, unsupported system mechanics,
copyright-like overlap, rights eligibility, and style limits.

Require 12–24 words for the essence, 90–140 words and 4–6 sentences for the summary, a non-empty
support set for every sentence, zero source quotations, and explicit unavailable records where
eligible evidence does not exist. Automated QA may mark a draft `qa-passed`; only a human editor may
mark it `human-approved` or change publication eligibility to `publishable`.
